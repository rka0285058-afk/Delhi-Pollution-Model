    Who bears it: district risk in

    RRI = risk relative to the whole-city 2025 observed average (1.00). Population, deaths, RRI and “P(top-3)” follow the selected year (P(top-3) = share of 1,000 random draws of weights, district PM2.5 pattern and water hazard in which the district is in the top three; it is recomputed in the browser for each year and scenario).

    Advanced assumptions

    Read this before quoting numbers. This is a relative-risk screening model, not a forecast or an official health estimate. Water pollution is represented by Yamuna BOD only (the one parameter with multi-year anchors); the 2010–2013 water values are held at the 2014 estimate and 2026 at 2025 because no annual data were retrieved. District air pattern and vulnerability are fixed at their 2025 / Census-2011 values for all years. The district informal-housing share is an illiteracy-based proxy. Full list below.
